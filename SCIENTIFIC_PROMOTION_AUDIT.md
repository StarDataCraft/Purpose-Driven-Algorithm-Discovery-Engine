# Scientific primary-idea promotion audit

## Reproduced failure

Build `3d1578d1` performed deterministic field-presence checks before ranking,
but the complete ten-angle result audit was created only after a primary idea
had already been committed. Its `EXPLORATORY` decision therefore annotated the
invalid Predictive Random Forest result without revoking primary status.

Direction-level evidence counts were also described as direct candidate support.
Canonical-family proximity, contextual papers, and irrelevant papers could
therefore inflate the displayed claim. Finally, drift experiments inherited
`expert activation accuracy` from a task template even when the candidate had no
expert state or action.

## Promotion order after repair

Draft candidates now receive conservative paper roles, an executable
`AlgorithmModificationSpec`, lightweight scientific gates, and the full
multi-angle audit before ranking. A failed or unavailable mandatory audit makes
the candidate ineligible. Only the remaining eligible pool can be ranked and
committed to Part 3.

The hard gates cover exact problem and metric fit, direct failure evidence,
known-solution status, operational mechanism fields, structural alignment,
information availability, implementation completeness, recurrence-specific
causality, experiment consistency, falsification, and the full audit decision.

One bounded recovery run is attempted when every draft fails. It changes search
coverage but never lowers a gate or silently changes retrieval mode. If it still
finds no eligible candidate, Part 2 shows an explicit no-defensible-primary state
and the strongest draft as an exploratory, non-promoted concept. Part 3 remains
unavailable.

## Predictive Random Forest regression

The mandatory fixture contains the displayed observer equation, ordinary
prediction-time inputs without labels, a surface-similarity audit, insufficient
prior-art coverage, one weak contextual paper, and two unrelated papers.

It is not promoted. Recorded failures include:

- full critical-audit rejection;
- unsupported generic Random Forest binding for online drift;
- undefined `F`, `H`, `K`, and `y` symbols and no concrete forest action;
- unavailable true-label residual at ordinary prediction time;
- absent recurrence-specific state-reuse path;
- expert-activation metric leakage despite no expert mechanism.

The hardware and Chimera papers are `IRRELEVANT`, excluded from default support,
and the direct-support count is at most one rather than five.

## Offline benchmark outcomes

- Recurring drift: 3 directions, 2 drafts, no promoted primary after one bounded
  recovery. Both drafts lacked valid direct evidence and recurrence causality;
  the generic Random Forest binding and incomplete symbolic update also failed.
- Missingness shift: 1 direction, 2 drafts, no promoted primary. Prior-art and
  structural-audit coverage remained below mandatory thresholds; one draft also
  required unavailable inference information.
- Dynamic cluster birth/death: 2 directions, 2 drafts, no promoted primary.
  Direct evidence and prior-art coverage were insufficient, with one additional
  structural/information failure.

These are intentionally honest outcomes: the benchmark fixtures do not provide
enough evidence to manufacture one validated primary idea per task.
